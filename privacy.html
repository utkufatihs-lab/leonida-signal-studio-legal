Leonida Signal Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how Leonida Signal Studio handles information when you connect TikTok or other platforms.

What we collect

  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok (and other platforms) to publish content you approve.
  • Basic public profile information returned by authorized APIs (e.g. display name, avatar).
  • Videos, titles, and captions you choose to publish through the studio.

How we use it

Only to operate the studio: authenticate, upload scheduled posts, and show connection status. We do not sell personal data.

Storage

Tokens are stored encrypted on the operator’s local machine / private server, not shared with third parties except the relevant platform APIs.

Your choices

You can disconnect TikTok in the developer portal or revoke access in TikTok account settings. Delete tokens locally by removing OAuth data in the studio.
